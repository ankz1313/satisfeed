# Shot prompt template

Build each prompt from the theme in `config/themes.json` like this (one paragraph, plain language):

```
<camera>. <hands> <action from shot_ideas> <subject>. <motion>.
Lighting: <lighting>. Colour palette: <palette>.
Sound: <sound_cues>.
<style_rules joined with "; ">.
```

## Example — puzzle, shot 3

> Locked-off overhead top-down shot with a slow macro push-in. A pair of relaxed adult hands with short clean nails, no rings, sleeves out of frame, press a jigsaw piece down until it clicks into place on a light oak wooden table. Slow deliberate movements, one piece at a time. Lighting: soft diffused morning window light, gentle shadows, warm neutral tones. Colour palette: muted pastels, cream, sage green, dusty blue. Sound: soft cardboard click as the piece seats, quiet room tone. Hands only, no faces; slow, calm, satisfying motion; no text, captions, logos or watermarks; photorealistic; steady camera, no fast cuts.

## Example — squishy, shot 2

> Close-up macro at a slight high angle, shallow depth of field, steady tripod. A pair of relaxed adult hands with short clean nails, no rings, sleeves out of frame, release a soft pastel pink slow-rising foam squishy and it slowly rises back to shape on a matte surface. Lighting: soft diffused studio light, creamy bokeh background. Colour palette: pastel pink, peach, lavender, mint. Sound: soft squish and gentle foam crackle, quiet room tone. Hands only, no faces; slow, calm, satisfying motion; no text, captions, logos or watermarks; photorealistic; steady camera, no fast cuts.

## Tips
- Keep the object identical across shots (same colour, same puzzle image) so the crossfades feel like one scene.
- End on the most satisfying moment (last puzzle piece, slowest rise of the foam).
